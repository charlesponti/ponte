What I need from you

  1. A, B or C. My recommendation is A — the truncated ellipsis rows and the settings rows are the same language, and a buyer who has used a Mac already knows where to click.
  2. Does the window keep one name? A makes "Ponte" the window title with Dictations as a pane; B calls it "Dictations" and hides settings behind a button. You said history should be where everything lives — A is that, literally.
  3. 860 × 560, or stay at 780 × 500? Grown for the sidebar and a settings pane that does not scroll.
  4. Vocabulary as a pane, or just a button that opens the file? The board shows the pane: seeing the two rules beats a menu item that opens JSON.

Nothing here is built. This board is one file — design/settings-window.html — and no app binary, no install and no permission was touched while it was made. The hotkey keys, the 46 dictations, their durations and their text are read from this Mac's own files; the microphone note is written for the case the pin falls back, which is issue #14.
