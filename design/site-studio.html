Three moves, no window to find.

⌥⌘Space

One hotkey, any app. The orb appears — listening. No window to find, no menu to open.

Talk.

The dots answer your voice, pushing outward with every word and shrinking between them, while the pill counts the seconds.

It types.

The transcript drops at your cursor. Mail, Notes, Slack, Terminal — no copy and paste, no switching windows.

A window for everything you said.

Every dictation, by day

⌘, opens a window with your takes, honest totals per day, and any one of them readable in full.

Hold the key, not tap it

Hold ⌥⌘Space while you speak and release to finish. A lost key-up ends itself at five minutes.

Choose the input

Pin a device and Ponte records from it; unplug it and the take says which microphone it actually used.

No dead air, no dead line

Silence at either end never reaches the engine, and the line says “listening…” instead of looking broken.
