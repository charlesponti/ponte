LayoutOrb, then one row: the clock, then the transcript. The clock keeps its place from variant B, so the pill still answers "listening, and for how long" at a glance, and the line beneath it answers "what did it hear".
The lineOne line, never wraps. Older words slide off to the left behind a 34 pt fade rather than a hard cut. About the last eight words are legible at 12.5 pt in 330 pt.
Blur-inPer word: blur(5px) → 0, opacity 0 → 1, translateY(7px) → 0, scale(.96) → 1. Nothing moves unless a word arrives, so a pause looks like a pause.
InkThe newest word or two sit at 66 % and settle to 96 % once the next snapshot agrees. This is the honest part: each snapshot re-transcribes the audio so far, so a word can still change. A provisional word that looks final reads as a mistake; one that looks provisional reads as thinking.
CadenceSnapshot every ~1.2 s for the first half-minute, stretching after that — the engine cost grows with the take (0.59 s at 8 s of audio, about 2.5 s at two minutes). Past a minute the line is a courtesy rather than a read-out.
UnchangedThe paste, the note, and the target application. The live text exists only inside this pill; nothing reaches the app you are writing into until you stop.
