Why the dots don’t shed. When the orb is the thing shrinking, the whole object scales — sphere and dots together — so the lattice keeps its proportions and the orb dissolves rather than thins out. Dot count only had to drop in the earlier board, where a fixed-size orb was being compared at different diameters.

One decision worth your eye: the collapse makes the box wider than tall at the end (a 96 px square becomes a wide short pill). That is what “text + 16 px all round” produces. If you would rather it stayed square-ish, the fix is more vertical padding, not less — say so and I will show both.
