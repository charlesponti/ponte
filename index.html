01 — Press

⌥⌘Space

One hotkey. Any app. The orb appears — mint, spinning, listening. No window to find, no menu to open.

02 — Speak

Talk.

The orb's dots answer your voice — pushing outward with every word, shrinking in the silence between, while the pill counts the seconds out loud.

03 — Transcribe

It types.

Parakeet on the Neural Engine, in-process. No cloud, no account, no subscription — the model lives on the Neural Engine and the words never leave the machine.

And when it hears nothing — the microphone asleep, a room too quiet — the take is kept. Transcribe the last take again from the menu, and it runs the same recording through once more. Nothing is lost to one bad attempt.

Anywhere.
