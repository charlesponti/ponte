No cloud. No eavesdropping.

Speech recognition runs locally on Apple Silicon. Ponte downloads its speech model the first time you use it and checks for its own app updates. Your audio and transcript aren’t sent with those checks.

A few good questions.

Does Ponte transcribe while I speak?

No. Ponte records while you talk, then transcribes and pastes the text when you stop.

What does the first launch download?

The Parakeet speech model, about 600 MB. It stays on your Mac for future dictation.

Which Macs can run Ponte?

Ponte requires macOS 15 or later and Apple silicon.

Does it need an account or subscription?

No account or subscription. Ponte 1.1.0 is free to download.
